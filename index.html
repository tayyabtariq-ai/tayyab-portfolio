ABOUT ME

EXPERTISE

Most clinics don't lose patients over the care they give. They lose them at the front desk — a call that rings out at lunchtime, a reminder nobody sent, a follow-up that never happened.

I build the automation that closes those gaps: appointment booking that keeps working when the phone doesn't get picked up, reminders that go out on schedule, follow-up that happens whether or not anyone remembers, and patient support workflows that answer routine questions before they reach your staff.

Everything runs on n8n, connected to Google Calendar, Slack and Google Places — tools your clinic either already uses or never has to see. A working system is live in 4–5 business days.

After launch we stay on a weekly review call to tune the system around what actually happens at your front desk. When something breaks, you get me — not a ticket queue.
